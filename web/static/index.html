Three Autonomous Competition Engines

Each competition is an independent brain owning its format rules, tiebreaker criteria, and progression logic. The shared kernel provides mathematical predictions and simulation without enforcing tournament formats.

FIFA WORLD CUP 2026

48 TEAMS • 104 MATCHES

The expanded 48-nation mega tournament across Canada, Mexico, and the United States. Features 12 groups of 4 teams (72 round-robin fixtures).

The top 2 teams from each group plus the 8 best 3rd-placed qualifiers (governed by FIFA Annex-C 495-combination tables) advance into the 32-team single-elimination knockout bracket through to the Final.

CHAMPIONS LEAGUE 2026/27

36 CLUBS • SWISS LEAGUE

The revolutionary 36-club Swiss-system single league table. Each club contests 8 matches against 8 distinct opponents (4 home, 4 away).

Ranks 1–8 qualify directly for the Round of 16; ranks 9–24 contest two-legged knockout playoffs; ranks 25–36 are eliminated. Knockout ties use two-legged aggregate score, extra time, and penalty math.

LALIGA EA SPORTS 2026/27

20 CLUBS • 380 FIXTURES

The 20-club Spanish top-flight double round-robin campaign spanning 380 fixtures. Resolves league title, UEFA Champions League berths, and relegation battles.

Standings adhere strictly to official RFEF tiebreakers: points, head-to-head points, head-to-head goal difference, overall goal difference, and fair play points.

5-Signal Ensemble & Bivariate Poisson Engine

Match probabilities are produced by an ensemble engine blending five independent signals with inverse log-loss calibration weights, combined with a Poisson expected-goals distribution.

01 // REFINED ELO RATINGS

CLUBELO + ELORATINGS

Continuous dynamic rating system updated from verified match results. Incorporates home field advantage, goal differential scaling, and tournament importance coefficients.

02 // MARKET ODDS CONSENSUS

THE ODDS API

Multi-bookmaker consensus probabilities stripped of bookmaker margins (overround/vigorish) via additive and proportional normalizations to yield true implied probabilities.

03 // ROLLING FORM

EXPONENTIAL DECAY

Recency-weighted momentum model tracking team points, goal differential, and opponent quality over the preceding 5 to 10 fixtures with exponential decay.

04 // SQUAD VALUATION

TRANSFERMARKT DATA

Log-ratio player and squad market valuation model reflecting squad depth, bench strength, and talent disparities between competing clubs and national sides.

05 // REST DAYS & FATIGUE

CALENDAR CONGESTION

Non-linear recovery model evaluating inter-match turnaround times, travel distances, and squad fatigue penalties during congested fixture windows.

06 // POISSON & MONTE CARLO

1 TO 1,000,000 RUNS

Bivariate Poisson distribution mapping expected goals (λ) across all exact scorelines, powering an isolated, seeded Monte Carlo repetition engine for tournament brackets.

Truthful Ingestion Guarantees & Baseline Edge

FOOTBALL does not guess, fabricate numbers, or blend simulated data into factual stores. Every computation follows strict architectural contracts.

FACTUAL VS. SIMULATED PROVENANCE

STRICT DATA ENUMS

Played matches are immutable historical facts (`MatchStatus.PLAYED`). They are never simulated or overridden. Unplayed matches carry explicit `MatchStatus.SCHEDULED`.

Every simulated number is tagged with `ResultProvenance.SIMULATED` and quarantined from canonical factual stores.

AUTOMATIC SIMULATED RETIREMENT

ZERO STALE NUMBERS

The instant real matchday results land from live providers, simulated projections for those fixtures are permanently retired.

No view ever presents outdated hypothetical numbers once factual match outcomes become available.

ZERO PROVIDER CALLS AT BOOT

LAZY ON-DEMAND INGEST

Server boot makes zero provider network calls. Ingestion is strictly explicit and on-demand. Offline snapshot mode runs with 100% functionality and zero network requests.

EMPIRICALLY BENCHMARKED BASELINE

LOG-LOSS VALIDATED

Prediction accuracy is evaluated empirically via multi-class log-loss and Brier score against naive baselines, rather than asserted.

Historical backtesting across seasons guarantees probability calibration before production deployment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the core methodologies, data provenance boundaries, and operational guarantees.

How does the system ensure simulated predictions never overwrite real results?

Played matches are stored with immutable MatchStatus.PLAYED status. The simulation engine only evaluates unplayed matches (MatchStatus.SCHEDULED) and tags all generated outcomes with ResultProvenance.SIMULATED. Factual and simulated states reside in separate data stores.

What happens when an external data provider is unavailable or down?

The platform operates with a 100% offline-first guarantee. Every competition includes a verified, pre-committed local snapshot dataset. If external APIs fail or are rate-limited, the system falls back gracefully to cached snapshots with an explicit STALE indicator.

How often are live match scores and standings refreshed?

The browser application performs automated background polling every 60 seconds with instant catch-up when returning to the tab. Furthermore, an automated GitHub Actions keep-awake workflow triggers periodic synchronization, ensuring data freshness.

How are match probabilities calculated and blended?

Probabilities originate from five independent signals: Refined Elo ratings, Consensus Market Odds, Rolling Form, Squad Valuation, and Rest Days. These signals are blended by an EnsembleEngine using inverse log-loss calibration weights fitted to historical out-of-sample matches.

Can I run Monte Carlo simulations with a custom seed for reproducible results?

Yes. The interactive simulation panel exposes both iteration count (1 to 1,000,000) and an optional random seed input. Providing a fixed integer seed guarantees byte-for-byte identical tournament progression and champion probability distributions.
